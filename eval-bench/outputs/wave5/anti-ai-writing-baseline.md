# Anti-AI Writing Audit — Baseline (no skill)

## Input text

> "Welcome to our innovative platform! We're excited to delve into a comprehensive solution that elevates your workflow. Our cutting-edge tool isn't just powerful — it's transformative. Whether you're a startup or an enterprise, we've got you covered. Let's unlock the potential of your team together."

## Patterns identified

The text contains several AI-writing markers:

- **"innovative platform"** — generic promotional adjective with no substance
- **"delve into"** — classic AI vocabulary tell
- **"comprehensive solution"** — vague corporate filler
- **"elevates your workflow"** — inflated promotional language
- **"cutting-edge"** — overused promotional adjective
- **"not just powerful — it's transformative"** — negative parallelism structure
- **"Whether you're a startup or an enterprise"** — forced false range
- **"Let's unlock the potential"** — announcement/signposting phrase

## Suggested rewrite

"This platform reduces the steps between your idea and the final output. It handles both one-person projects and multi-team rollouts. You can start in 10 minutes."

## Verdict

**Rewrite.** The original reads like a landing page generated by an AI prompted to "write exciting copy." Nearly every sentence contains at least one AI-writing tell.
